# The 24 runes of the Elder Futhark, matched to the shared archetypes (lib/archetypes-data.ts).
# DRAFT written 2026-10-15 by Claude for the user to review. Nothing reads this file yet: it is not in the database.
# Format and the meaning of each basis: lib/symbol-archetype-map.ts. Order matters: the first link is the primary one.
#
# How to read a link:  slug:aspect=basis.  "poem" means one of the three old rune poems (Old English, Norwegian, Icelandic)
# says something on that theme, judged from the one-line summaries already in lib/runes-data.ts (paraphrased for this app,
# not quoted). The Old English poem covers 29 runes of the Anglo-Saxon row, so it can speak to Elder Futhark runes the
# Norwegian and Icelandic poems (16 runes each) do not. "name" is the reconstructed meaning of the rune's name.
# "modern" is divination practice (Blum 1982 and later); the old poems give no divination meanings and no reversed ones.
#
# Every link that was in lib/common-meanings-data.ts is kept; a few new links come from what a poem says (see git history
# for which). Nothing was removed. The poems describe what a rune stands for; the app's
# light and shadow wording is the app's. Where the poems disagree with each other, the note says so.
#
# Bases and the sources behind them. There is no source card yet for Blum 1982; the user may want one (see the audit).

rune-fehu | wealth-prosperity=poem, generosity-giving=poem, anger-conflict:shadow=poem, work-craft=modern | OE: wealth comforts everyone but should be shared. Norwegian and Icelandic: wealth causes quarrels among kin. Work and craft is modern only.
rune-uruz | courage-strength=poem, energy-vitality=name, healing-health=modern | OE: the aurochs is fierce and fights with its horns. The Norwegian and Icelandic verses (slag, drizzle) do not support the theme; it rests on the OE verse and the name (aurochs, debated). Healing is modern.
rune-thurisaz | protection-boundaries=poem, struggle-resilience=modern, anger-conflict=modern, harm-abuse:shadow=poem | OE: the thorn is sharp and painful to grasp (a guard that hurts). Norwegian and Icelandic: the giant brings sickness and torment to women (the app records what the poems say; it does not endorse it). The name is read as thorn or giant; scholars differ.
rune-ansuz | communication-expression=poem, learning-wisdom=poem, messages-news=modern, intuition-inner-knowing=modern, spirituality-faith=name | OE: the source of speech and a support of wisdom (the word can mean god or mouth; scholars disagree). Icelandic: an old god. The link to spirituality comes from the name. Messages and intuition are modern.
rune-raidho | travel-journeys=poem, struggle-resilience=poem, progress-momentum=modern, patience-waiting=modern | OE and Icelandic: riding is easy for those indoors and toil for horse and rider on the long road. Norwegian: the worst thing for horses.
rune-kenaz | energy-vitality=poem, creativity-talent=modern, learning-wisdom=modern, healing-health:shadow=poem | OE: the torch burns with a bright flame. Norwegian and Icelandic: the name there is a sore or ulcer (the poems and the name disagree here, and the modern torch comes from the OE reading).
rune-gebo | generosity-giving=poem, partnership-marriage=modern, balance-moderation=modern | OE only (Gebo is not in the Younger Futhark): giving brings honour and helps those with nothing. Partnership is modern, from the idea of exchange in both directions.
rune-wunjo | joy-contentment=poem, peace-harmony=modern, community-belonging=modern | OE only: joy belongs to someone with few troubles, comfort and a good house.
rune-hagalaz | change-transformation=poem, disaster-collapse=modern, struggle-resilience=modern | OE: hail falls and turns to water. Norwegian and Icelandic: cold grain, sleet. The theme of sudden harm is modern; the poems stress the cold and the melting.
rune-nauthiz | struggle-resilience=poem, healing-health=poem, acceptance-surrender=modern, wealth-prosperity:shadow=app | OE: need is hard on the heart, yet heeded in time it turns to help and healing. Icelandic: hardship and toilsome work. Wealth (as lack of it) is this app's own link and has no source.
rune-isa | patience-waiting=name, rest-selfcare=modern, solitude-reflection=modern, progress-momentum:shadow=app | The name is ice: a standstill. OE: ice is cold and slippery; Norwegian: a bridge the blind must be led over. The link to rest and reflection is modern; progress as the hard side (a stop) is the app's own.
rune-jera | nature-earth=poem, hope-optimism=poem, work-craft=name, patience-waiting=modern, success-recognition=modern | OE: the year is a hope to people when the earth gives its fruits to rich and poor alike. All three poems: a boon, a good harvest. The name is year or harvest.
rune-eihwaz | struggle-resilience=poem, protection-boundaries=poem, endings-release=modern, death-mortality=modern, spirituality-faith=modern | OE: the yew is hard and firm in the earth, a guardian of fire. Yew and death is modern here (the old poems do not say it); the Norwegian and Icelandic poems have no verse for this rune.
rune-perthro | play-humour=poem, fate-luck-karma=poem, mystery-secrets=modern, compulsion-addiction:shadow=app | OE: unclear; translators read a game piece or dice cup, a source of play and laughter, so chance follows only if that reading is right. The name is unknown. Mystery and compulsion are modern or the app's own.
rune-algiz | protection-boundaries=poem, spirituality-faith=modern, intuition-inner-knowing=modern | OE: elk-sedge wounds anyone who grasps at it (a plant that defends itself). The name is uncertain. The Norwegian and Icelandic verses are for the later rune yr, not this one.
rune-sowilo | hope-optimism=poem, support-guidance=poem, success-recognition=modern, energy-vitality=modern | OE: the sun is a hope to sailors until they reach land. Norwegian: the sun lights the world. Icelandic: a shining ray.
rune-tiwaz | loyalty-trust-betrayal=poem, acceptance-surrender=myth, justice-law=modern, courage-strength=modern, truth-honesty-integrity=modern | OE: a guiding sign that keeps faith with nobles. The Norwegian and Icelandic poems say Tyr has one hand; the sacrifice reading comes from the story of Tyr and the wolf Fenrir, told in the Prose Edda, not in the poems. Justice and honesty are modern.
rune-berkano | growth-maturity=poem, fertility-birth=modern, parenthood-caregiving=modern, family-home=modern | OE: the birch bears no fruit but sends up shoots. Norwegian: the greenest shrub. Icelandic: a leafy twig, a young tree. Birth and home are modern.
rune-ehwaz | travel-journeys=poem, progress-momentum=name, partnership-marriage=modern, loyalty-trust-betrayal=modern | OE only: the horse is a joy to nobles, proud on its hooves, a comfort to the restless. Partnership and trust are modern (horse and rider).
rune-mannaz | community-belonging=poem, identity-individuality=name, death-mortality=poem, loyalty-trust-betrayal:shadow=poem, growth-maturity=modern | OE: a person is dear to kin, yet each will fail the other, and the body goes to the earth. Norwegian: a person is the increase of the dust.
rune-laguz | fear-anxiety=poem, nature-earth=poem, intuition-inner-knowing=modern, emotional-expression=modern, dreams-vision=modern | OE: water seems endless to people on an unsteady ship, and the waves frighten them. Norwegian and Icelandic: a waterfall, a geyser, a land full of fish. Intuition and feeling are modern.
rune-ingwaz | travel-journeys=poem, fertility-birth=myth, endings-release=modern, rest-selfcare=modern, new-beginnings=modern | OE only: Ing was first seen among the East Danes, then went east over the sea. Ing as a fertility god is argued from the wider tradition, not from this verse, and scholars differ.
rune-dagaz | hope-optimism=poem, messages-news=poem, intellect-clarity=poem, change-transformation=modern, new-beginnings=modern | OE only: day is a messenger, dear to people, a bright light and a hope and a help to all.
rune-othala | family-home=poem, tradition-convention=poem, security-stability=poem, past-memory=modern, community-belonging=modern | OE only: an estate is dear to everyone who can enjoy there, in peace and plenty, what is right and proper.
