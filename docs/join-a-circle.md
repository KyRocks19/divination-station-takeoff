# Joining a circle

Someone you know runs Divination Station on their computer and has invited you to their circle. You do not download the app. You open it in a browser, on a phone, a tablet or any computer, and their computer does the work. A small or old device is fine.

Your host sends you two things with this note: **the address** (it looks like `https://their-pc.tail1234.ts.net`) and **a circle code**.

## Before you join: what goes where
- Everything you write in the app (readings, journal entries, forum posts) is stored on your host's computer, not on your device.
- It is not end-to-end encrypted, so **your host can read it**. Join only a circle whose host you trust with your writing.
- Your private readings and journal are never shown to the other members. Forum posts and comments are shown to everyone in the circle.
- The app shows you this again before you join, under "Before you join: what goes where".

## 1. Install Tailscale
The circle is not on the public internet. Your host's computer is reached through Tailscale, a free private network.
1. Install the Tailscale app from tailscale.com, or from your phone's app store.
2. Make a Tailscale account and sign in.
3. Tell your host the email address of that account. They share their computer with it.
4. Accept the share when Tailscale tells you about it (by email, or in the app).

Leave Tailscale switched on whenever you use the circle.

## 2. Make your account
1. Open the address your host sent you in your browser. Save it as a bookmark, or add it to your home screen.
2. On the sign-in page, choose **I have an invite code**.
3. Enter the circle code, a username and a password. Keep the password somewhere safe.
4. Read "Before you join: what goes where" and agree. You are in.

## If something goes wrong
- **The page does not open:** check that Tailscale is on and signed in to the account your host shared with, and that your host's computer is on and awake.
- **"This address is not one this copy of the app answers to":** the address is not exactly the one your host sent. Copy it again.
- **"This circle is full":** ask your host to make room.
- **You are signed out again at once:** the address must start with `https://`.

To leave the circle, ask your host to take you out. To keep your writing first, use Settings, Export.
