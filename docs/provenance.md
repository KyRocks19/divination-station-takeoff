# Provenance sheet: where everything in a download came from (open item ED18)

Prepared 2026-10-18 by Claude, from the repository, `NOTICE`, `docs/release-ladder.md` (task 1) and the owner's statements. **Not legal advice.** The rule from the release ladder: one line per item (what it is, who made it, under what terms); **an item marked "not sure" stays out of a download until the owner answers**, and goes back in when the answer is known. "Known" means a file, a commit or the owner's own words say so; it is not a check that nobody else holds rights.

How to read the last column: **In** = may go in a download as it is. **Hold** = not sure, leave it out (or ship a text-only or empty version) until answered. **Owner** = the owner's answer is what is missing.

## What a download can ship if items 3 and 4 stay on hold
The program, the card pictures, the archetype and tool data, the traditional notes and sources, the fonts and the drawings all work without them. The tarot card pages would show the card's picture and name, and the person's own words, with the written descriptions and the encyclopedia empty. The card names and the order and numbering of the 78 cards are the tradition's own and not in question. Whether the app is still worth running in that state is for the owner to judge; the "bring your own meanings" purpose of the app (the person is the author) suggests it is.
